In this mode the extension can't open local files on your computer. It sends the page's text and, for PDFs, a screenshot of what's on screen.

  


  
Page access

  

  
Grok Browser needs site access on all sites so the side panel can read whichever tab you're on and take a screenshot of it. If you limit that in the browser's extension settings, press Alt+Shift+G or click the toolbar icon on a tab before asking.

  
Screenshots: for PDFs and pages with little readable text, each question also includes a screenshot of what's on screen, so the model can see the page number and the passage you're on. If a screenshot can't be taken, the panel tells you why. In Grok Bot mode the screenshot stays on your relay for 15 minutes.

  
Local files (PDF, HTML opened from your disk): open chrome://extensions → Grok Browser → Details → turn on Allow access to file URLs.
